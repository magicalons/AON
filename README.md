# Sovereign and Global Web4 Cryptocurrency Ecosystem with PQC on Blockchain

This project defines a **strategic architecture** to establish a **Sovereign and Global Web4 Cryptocurrency Ecosystem**, with an initial focus on the **United States of America (USA)**.

Our vision is to position the **USA as the global capital of Web4**, merging physical and digital layers to create a **secure, decentralized, and sovereign technological infrastructure**, adaptable to any nation that values digital sovereignty.

---

## Main Features

### 1. Sovereign Blockchain Infrastructure
- **Layer 2 (L2)** network built on **Ethereum or Polygon**.
- Physical nodes distributed across participating countries to ensure **independence, resilience, and technological sovereignty**.

### 2. Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC)
- Integration of **quantum-resistant cryptographic protocols**.
- Based on **NIST PQC standards** (CRYSTALS-Kyber and CRYSTALS-Dilithium).
- Ensures long-term security against quantum computing threats.

### 3. 3FA Authentication with Artificial Intelligence
- **Triple-Factor Authentication (3FA)** system combining:
  1. Advanced biometrics  
  2. Secure hardware (FIDO3 PQC)  
  3. Contextual verification via AI  
- Prevents identity spoofing and ensures integrity in Web4 environments.

### 4. Decentralized Telecommunications
- **Community-managed network nodes** through DAO governance.  
- **Li-Fi protocol** for secure, high-speed communication in critical environments.  
- Reduces dependency on centralized infrastructures.

### 5. Legal and Governance Framework
- Governance through a **DAO with legal personality**.  
- **On-chain transparency** and compliance with **local and international regulations**.  
- Smart governance mechanisms and collective validation.

### 6. Economic Incentive Model
- **Tokenization of real and digital assets**.  
- Creation of a **sovereign data marketplace** for sustainable economic growth.  
- Incentives for participation, validation, and network contribution.
